Support

Something not working, or a question about the app? Open an issue and we will answer there.

I hear nothing when I record a USB instrument

While a take is running, iOS sends sound out through the instrument, not the speaker. Plug headphones or a speaker into the instrument to hear yourself. When you stop, playback returns to the speaker.

The app says “No input”

Allow the microphone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. If an instrument is plugged in, check that it is switched on and that its USB audio is enabled.

How do I clear a track?

Touch and hold the track’s key and choose Erase. To start over, open the tapes list with the gear and add a new tape.

How does the loop work?

Add cues where a section starts and ends, then switch loop on. The app repeats the section the playhead is in. Touch another section on the scale to move the loop there.

How do I get my recording out?

Press export. The app mixes the four tracks into one audio file and opens the share sheet. Your tapes are also in the Files app under Vibe Tapes.
